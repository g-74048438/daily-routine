A Productive Day in Adam's Life
Adam is a diligent Year 3 pupil. Every morning, he wakes up at 6:00 a.m. First, he makes his bed and washes his face. Next, he eats a delicious breakfast of eggs and milk with his sister at 6:30 a.m. At 7:15 a.m., Adam walks to school because his house is nearby. After school at 1:30 p.m., Adam comes home and eats lunch. In the afternoon at 4:00 p.m., he plays football with his friends in the garden. Then, he completes his homework at 7:00 p.m. because he wants to revise his lessons every day. Finally, Adam reads a bedtime story and goes to sleep peacefully at 9:00 p.m.
